### 2. COAXING STARTER

You could open by contrasting wholesale text generation with a drafting workflow in which you lay down unorganized ideas while background agents check factual claims, ease writer's block, and present reasoned edits through accept-or-deny prompts. From that concrete starting point, you can immediately introduce the central tension you noticed between preserving your own wording against generic cadence and resisting the pull to auto-accept machine revisions.

### 3. DRAFTED PARAGRAPH

Artificial intelligence writing tools rarely push authors completely out of the loop, yet background fact-checking and permission prompts hold writers on the loop only precariously. In Nandita Biswas Mellamphy's framework distinguishing human mastery in the loop from total displacement out of the loop, an on-the-loop relation treats humans and machines as "co-producers under specific but changing environmental conditions that are conceptualized as being both within and beyond human control" (Mellamphy 2021: 20). This lens fits a writer drafting unorganized ideas with background agents that catch factual errors, ease writer's block, and explain proposed edits for approval, although control remains partial. Matt Carlson deepens this tension by asking "whether an increase in algorithmic judgment will lead to a decline in the authority of human judgment" (Carlson 2015: 429). As the writer recognizes when resisting heavy editing that introduces generic cadence, delegating verification and revision to automated editors can quietly erode voice and epistemic confidence. Luke Munn further complicates incremental optimism by showing that even where automation leaves human labor intact, "these technologies shape workers, steering their practices and exerting intense social, cognitive, and psychological pressures on them" (Munn 2022: 30). Under persistent cognitive strain, authors using permission prompts can easily drift into habitual auto-acceptance. Ultimately, interactive oversight preserves more shared agency than wholesale text generation does, yet holding the line requires sustained editorial vigilance.
